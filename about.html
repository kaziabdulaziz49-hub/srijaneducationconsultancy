Who We Are

Srijan Education Consultancy (SEC) is a trusted educational consultancy and career guidance organization established in 2016 with a mission to help students achieve their academic and professional dreams. Over the years, SEC has become one of the most reliable names in education counselling, admission guidance, career planning, scholarship assistance, and student support services.

We understand that choosing the right course, college, or career path is one of the most important decisions in a student's life. Our team works closely with students and parents to understand their goals, interests, strengths, and future aspirations. Through expert counselling and personalized guidance, we help students make informed decisions that lead to successful careers and bright futures.

Since our establishment, thousands of students have benefited from our services and secured admissions in reputed colleges and universities across India. Our dedication, transparency, and student-first approach have helped us build a strong reputation among students, parents, educational institutions, and industry professionals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide honest, reliable, and professional educational guidance to students from all backgrounds. We aim to bridge the gap between students and quality education by offering accurate information, career counselling, admission support, scholarship opportunities, and complete assistance throughout the educational journey.

We believe that every student possesses unique talents and potential. Our goal is to empower students by helping them discover their strengths, choose the right career path, and gain access to quality education that aligns with their ambitions.

Why Choose SEC?

At Srijan Education Consultancy, we focus on delivering genuine guidance and long-term support rather than simply processing admissions. We maintain strong relationships with educational institutions and provide students with updated information regarding admissions, entrance exams, scholarships, educational trends, and career opportunities.

Our experienced counsellors offer personalized attention to every student, ensuring that each individual receives guidance tailored to their specific goals. We also organize educational seminars, career awareness programs, scholarship drives, and student development activities throughout the year.
